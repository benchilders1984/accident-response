Accident Response

Job Site Emergency Instructions

Life-Threatening / Catastrophic Accident

CALL 911 NOW
  1. Stay with the injured person.
  2. Do not move them unless there is immediate danger.
  3. Send someone to direct emergency responders to the exact location.
  4. Call your Project Manager.
  5. Contact the Safety Team.
  6. Contact site supervision.

Non-Life-Threatening Injury

  1. Provide onsite care / first aid.
  2. Assess whether medical treatment is needed.
  3. If medical care is needed, the foreman drives the injured individual.
  4. Preferred clinic: Strada Occupational Health, 8630 F Street.
FIND STRADA OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH
  1. Call your Project Manager.
  2. Complete Accident Investigation and Eyewitness forms.
FIND NEAREST HOSPITAL
Use the hospital button when the preferred clinic is not appropriate, unavailable, or emergency care is needed.
